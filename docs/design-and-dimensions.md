## 2. Design Status

The metal frame has been fabricated. The pot-retaining rod attachments and rear fan mounts remain to be completed. The plan is to fabricate the attachments first and then update the SolidWorks model and engineering drawing to reflect the actual installed parts.

## 3. CAD and Drawing Workflow

1. Measure the fabricated frame, including outer dimensions, clear opening, depth and pipe wall thickness if accessible.
2. Fit the real pots and determine safe retaining points, spacing and clearances.
3. Fabricate and attach the rods; record actual rod count, cut length and attachment method.
4. Establish fan orientation, mounting-hole pattern, clearances, guards and airflow direction from the actual fan hardware.
5. Measure the completed assembly and update the SolidWorks model to match it.
6. Update the drawing dimensions and identify any differences from the original design.
7. Export a viewable drawing (PDF) and, if useful, a neutral 3D file such as STEP for the repository.

## 4. Design Questions to Close

- What is the measured wall thickness of the 20 mm square pipe?
- What are the actual pot height, outer diameter, opening diameter and mass?
- Does the chosen rod layout support each pot securely without cracking the clay?
- How will the fans be guarded and kept clear of water spray?
- Will the fans pull air through the pots, or will a shroud/plenum be needed to reduce bypass?
- What tub dimensions allow for pump submergence and runoff collection without flooding the frame?
- Where will water return to the reservoir, and how will the tub be removed for cleaning?
- Can the pump and fans be accessed separately for inspection and repair?
