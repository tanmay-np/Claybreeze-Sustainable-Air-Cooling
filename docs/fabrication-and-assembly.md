## Recommended Assembly Sequence

### 1. Inspect the Frame

Measure and record the as-built width, height, depth and clear opening. Check squareness, joints, wobble, sharp edges and coating condition. Resolve structural concerns before adding wet clay pots or powered components.

### 2. Fit the Clay Pots

Arrange the actual pots without forcing them into place. Check spacing, alignment, opening clearance and access for cleaning. Ceramic pots can crack if point-loaded or clamped too tightly; use suitable support or padding where necessary. Verify the total number of pots rather than relying on the provisional 5 × 6 arrangement.

### 3. Fabricate and Attach the Retaining Rods

Confirm the final rod layout, cut lengths, attachment locations and clearances from the actual pots and frame. Keep a record of the final measurements and attachment method. After attachment, inspect the joints and check that rods retain pots securely without putting damaging stress on them.

### 4. Fabricate and Attach Fan Mounts

Record each fan's outside dimensions, hole pattern, airflow direction, model and electrical rating. Mount the fans securely and fit appropriate guards over accessible moving blades. Check that the mounting arrangement does not obstruct the intended air path. Prevent water spray and runoff from reaching motors, connectors or controls.

### 5. Update CAD to Match the As-Built Assembly

After fabrication, measure the actual components and update the SolidWorks model and drawing. Mark provisional features as revised and do not represent unmeasured attachment geometry as final. Save dated versions or use Git commits to record significant revisions.

### 6. Install the Water Circuit

After confirming the pump, tubing and reservoir specifications, install the bottom tub, secure the pump, route the main tube to the top distributor and arrange the return path. Ensure the tub can be cleaned and removed safely. Check pump instructions for required submergence, maximum head, allowed fluid and inlet conditions.

### 7. Commission in Stages

1. With electrical equipment off, inspect mechanical assembly and all connections.
2. Test the water circuit in a controlled manner, checking every distribution outlet, leakage and return flow.
3. Switch off and isolate power before adjusting tubing or handling the wet assembly.
4. Test airflow with the water pump off and check fan direction, vibration, obstructions and bypass paths.
5. Only after separate inspections, test the combined system using suitable electrical protection and supervision.
6. Record any changes made and update the CAD and assembly log.
