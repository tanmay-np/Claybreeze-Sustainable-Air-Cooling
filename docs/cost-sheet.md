## How the estimates were made

- **MS frame length:** the SolidWorks GLB export (`assets/models/claybreeze-assembly.glb`, mesh `20mm_frame-1`) is a closed mesh of 2,773 cm³. It models the pipe as a solid 20 × 20 mm bar, so its volume ÷ 400 mm² gives the member length: **6.93 m** (front and back frames, depth members, rear fan-mount extension). With 10 % wastage: **7.63 m**.
- **MS frame mass:** because the CAD section is solid, volume × 7850 kg/m³ (21.8 kg) would overstate a hollow pipe. The wall thickness is not confirmed, so a **1.2 mm wall is assumed**: 90.24 mm² → 0.708 kg/m → **5.40 kg**.
- **Rods:** 6 × 480 mm of Ø12 mm round bar + 10 % = 3.17 m × 0.888 kg/m = **2.81 kg**.
- **Steel rate:** ₹60/kg, the midpoint of ₹55–65/kg for MS pipes ([OfBusiness](https://www.ofbusiness.com/prices/pipes)); the same rate is assumed for the round bar.
- **Electronics:** [Robocraze](https://robocraze.com/) listed prices on 28 Nov 2026 (ESP32 38-pin ₹455, DHT11 ₹62, 3-channel relay ₹227, MB102 breadboard ₹68, jumper wire set ₹142).
- **MDF:** nested area of the enclosure DXF (426 × 253 mm) + 10 % at ₹13.55/sq ft ([materialdepot.in](https://materialdepot.in/greenply-8-ft-x-4-ft-interior-grade-plain-mdf-3-mm/product)).
- **Consumables:** 0.5 kg of 2.5 mm electrodes at ₹165/kg ([TradeIndia](https://www.tradeindia.com/products/2-50-mm-arc-welding-electrode-rod-6840372.html)) and four 4-inch discs at ₹9 each ([IndiaMART](https://m.indiamart.com/yashtraders-gujarat)); the quantities are assumptions.

Market prices change; the estimated rows are indicative, not quotes.

## Next-stage material for water resistance (future scope)

- **MS + epoxy primer + powder coating:** lowest cost.
- **GI square pipe with cold-galvanised weld joints:** recommended.
- **Aluminium tube, bolted/riveted:** light, rust-free.
- **SS304:** most durable, highest cost.
